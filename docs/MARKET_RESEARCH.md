| # | Weakness | Mitigation |

| W4 | **High per-creation cost ($0.30–$2.00)** | Usage quotas, tiered gating, cost optimization |
| W5 | **Small team = limited bandwidth** | Prioritize ruthlessly; outsource non-core |
